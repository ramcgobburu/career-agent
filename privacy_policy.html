Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2024

1. Introduction

Career Agent ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our AI-powered career materials generation service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address (optional)
  • Name (optional)
  • API key (generated automatically for authentication)

2.2 Career Context Data

You may upload career context documents containing:

  • Work experience and employment history
  • Educational background
  • Skills and competencies
  • Professional achievements
  • Other career-related information you choose to provide

2.3 Usage Data

We collect information about how you use the service, including:

  • API requests and responses
  • Types of content generated (cover letters, blurbs, etc.)
  • Timestamps of requests

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our career materials generation service
  • Process your requests for personalized content generation
  • Authenticate your API requests
  • Store and manage your career context for personalized content creation

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored securely using industry-standard practices
  • API keys are encrypted and stored securely
  • Career context data is stored in isolated databases per user
  • We use secure connections (HTTPS) for all data transmission

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be processed by:

  • OpenAI: We use OpenAI's API to generate AI-powered content. Your career context may be sent to OpenAI for processing as part of the content generation service.
  • Service Providers: We use hosting services (Render) to operate our service. These providers have access to data necessary to provide the service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Export your career context data
To exercise these rights, contact us or use the API endpoints to manage your data.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time.

8. Third-Party Services

Our service uses OpenAI's API for content generation. Your use of our service is also subject to OpenAI's privacy policy and terms of service.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through GitHub: github.com/ramcgobburu/career-agent

11. OpenAI Actions

When using this GPT through OpenAI's platform, your interactions are also governed by OpenAI's privacy policy and terms of service.
